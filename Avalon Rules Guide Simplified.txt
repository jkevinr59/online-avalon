================================================================================
                         THE RESISTANCE: AVALON - RULES
================================================================================

Overview:
The Resistance: Avalon is a social deduction game for 5 to 10 players set in 
King Arthur's realm. Players are split into two hidden teams:
- Good (Loyal Servants of Arthur)
- Evil (Minions of Mordred)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OBJECTIVE & WIN CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Good Wins:
  * Completes 3 out of 5 Quests successfully AND protects Merlin from being
    assassinated at the end of the game.

- Evil Wins:
  * Fails 3 Quests.
  * Correctly identifies and assassinating Merlin after Good completes 3 Quests.
  * Reaches 5 consecutive rejected Team proposals in a single round.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PLAYER DISTRIBUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Players | Good | Evil
----------------------
   5    |  3   |  2
   6    |  4   |  2
   7    |  4   |  3
   8    |  5   |  3
   9    |  6   |  3
  10    |  6   |  4

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ROLES & ABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base Roles:
- Merlin (Good): Knows all Evil players (except Mordred), but must guide Good 
  subtly without revealing his identity.
- Assassin (Evil): Knows the Evil team. Gets one final attempt to guess and 
  assassinate Merlin if Good completes 3 Quests.
- Loyal Servants / Minions: Regular Good/Evil players without special information.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. NIGHT PHASE (SETUP & SCRIPT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before starting Round 1, all players close their eyes. A designated player 
reads the setup script:

1. "Minions of Mordred, open your eyes and look around so you 
   know each other." -> [Pause, then Close eyes]
2. "Minions of Mordred, stick out your thumbs so Merlin knows 
   you." -> "Merlin, open your eyes." -> [Pause, Merlin closes eyes, thumbs down]
3. "Everyone, open your eyes."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ROUND FLOW (5 QUESTS TOTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each round consists of two main phases:

Phase 1: Team Proposal & Voting
1. Selection: The current Leader nominates a team using the required team size.
2. Discussion: All players discuss the team composition.
3. Voting: Every player secretly chooses an Approve or Reject vote token. All 
   votes are revealed simultaneously.
   - Approved: Requires a strict majority (e.g., 3/5, 4/6). Proceed to Phase 2.
   - Rejected / Tied: The Leader token passes clockwise to the next player, 
     and team proposal repeats.
   - Note: If 5 team proposals in a row are rejected, Evil wins immediately.

Phase 2: Quest Execution
Only players selected for the Quest receive a pair of Success and Fail cards.
- Good players MUST play a Success card.
- Evil players MAY choose to play a Success or Fail card.

Cards are played face-down, thoroughly shuffled by the Leader, and revealed:
- Success: All played cards are Success.
- Fail: 1 Fail card is enough to fail the Quest (except Mission 4 in 7+ player 
  games).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. QUEST REQUIREMENTS & TEAM SIZES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Players | Quest 1 | Quest 2 | Quest 3 | Quest 4 | Quest 5
----------------------------------------------------------
   5    |    2    |    3    |    2    |    3    |    3
   6    |    2    |    3    |    4    |    3    |    4
   7    |    2    |    3    |    3    |   4*    |    4
   8    |    3    |    4    |    4    |   5*    |    5
   9    |    3    |    4    |    4    |   5*    |    5
  10    |    3    |    4    |    4    |   5*    |    5

* Special Rule (7+ Players, Quest 4): Requires 2 Fail cards to fail the quest 
  instead of 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ASSASSINATION PHASE (ENDGAME)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If Good completes 3 successful Quests:
1. Good does not win immediately.
2. The Evil team unmasks and openly discusses to decide who they think Merlin is.
3. The Assassin points to one Good player:
   - If that player is Merlin, Evil steals the victory.
   - If that player is NOT Merlin, Good wins.
